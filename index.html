PRESENTER NOTES · A QUICK TOUR

What Gather can do.

Use this written outline while presenting. Each section expands for a short feature summary, and links jump to the matching part of the site.

Finding and exploring staysSearch tools, destinations, comparison, favourites, and the map6 talking points
  • Search: Find a stay by Philippine destination, check-in and check-out dates, and group size, including up to 20 guests.Show search
  • Destinations: Browse featured places and stays across island, beach, city, mountain, heritage, farm, and nature categories.Browse stays
  • Compare: Sort by Gather picks, price, guest rating, or reviews. Filter by stay type, nightly budget, and minimum rating.Show filters
  • Stay details: Open a stay to review its description, amenities, capacity, estimated price, and dates.Open stay search
  • Favourites: Signed-in guests can save places and filter to see saved stays together.Show stays
  • Interactive map: Markers show approximate stay locations. Click a marker for stay details; expand the map to explore the Philippines more easily.Open expanded map
Booking and managing tripsAvailability checks, booking requests, and My trips3 talking points
  • Availability: Gather checks the details currently on file, but the property must verify dates and terms before accepting a request.Choose a stay
  • Booking request: Review dates, guests, and an indicative total. Your request stays pending until the property confirms it.Start a request
  • My trips: Signed-in guests can review the status of their booking requests and cancel a request.Open My trips
Hosting a placeListings, approximate map locations, availability, and reservations4 talking points
  • Create a listing: Hosts can add a place name, category, description, guest capacity, nightly price, amenities, and photos.Open host tools
  • Set the location: Choose an approximate location on the Philippines map or provide coordinates for the public map marker.Show host tools
  • Manage availability: Set eligible check-in days and the minimum stay, then edit your listing as needed.Manage a place
  • Review requests: Hosts can see requests associated with their listings and contact guests directly to verify details.Open host dashboard
AI help and booking informationTrip assistance, account access, and booking request expectations3 talking points
  • AI booking agent: Ask for recommendations using Gather’s listed stays, discuss trip details, or prepare a host listing draft.Open Gather chat
  • Booking with AI: The assistant can collect dates and guest count, but Gather checks the details on file and saves only a pending request after guest confirmation.Show the assistant
  • Booking requests: A request is saved in Gather, not sent to the property or confirmed. Contact the property directly to verify availability and terms; no payment is collected on Gather.
Tools and services behind GatherThe technology and online services used on the site6 talking points
  • Website and publishing: Gather uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and is published with GitHub Pages.
  • Accounts and shared data: Supabase Auth, Postgres, and Storage support accounts, listings, bookings, favourites, availability, and host-uploaded photos.
  • AI chat: A Supabase Edge Function securely connects Gather to its configured Groq AI service; provider credentials stay in server-side secrets.
  • Booking email: EmailJS sends configured booking-request notifications to the owner’s Gmail inbox for registered guests.
  • Interactive map: Leaflet displays Esri map tiles, with OpenStreetMap attribution and a Philippines boundary from geoBoundaries, OCHA Philippines, and NAMRIA.
  • Photography: Stay photos are stored in the project; the home-page hero image loads from Unsplash. Images are illustrative and may not show the listed stay.

Short intro to read aloud: “Gather helps travelers explore places to stay across the Philippines, compare details, and send booking requests. Guests can keep trip plans together, while hosts can manage their listings. The map is clickable and expandable, and the AI assistant can help explore the available stays.”

A QUICK NOTE BEFORE YOU EXPLORE

Find your place in the Philippines.

Browse freely. We’ll ask you to sign in only when you’re ready to request a stay or manage an account.

Booking information
  • Listings, rates, ratings, and availability are provided for discovery and may not reflect current property information.
  • A booking request is not a confirmed reservation. It is saved in Gather, not sent to the property; contact the property directly to confirm availability and terms.
  • Gather is not affiliated with listed properties unless a listing says otherwise. Photos may represent the destination, not the exact property.
  • Do not submit payment details. Any payment or final terms must be confirmed directly with the property.
